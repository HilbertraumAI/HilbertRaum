Leg 2: Qwen3.8 27B start with MTP, ABAB against b11146
------------------------------------------------------
From the repo root, variants in this order: b12110-a, b11146-a, b12110-b, b11146-b:
  node eval/results/hardware/i9-9900x-rtx-3090-24gb-128gb/run-start.mjs --leg 729 --variant mtp-<variant> \
    --model <root>/models/chat/qwen3.8-27b-ud-q5km.gguf --id qwen3.8-27b-ud-q5km --ctx 8192 \
    --mtp on --extra "--cache-ram 8192" --bin <root>/runtime/llama.cpp/linux/llama-server \
    --out <scratch> --slug i9-9900x-rtx-3090-24gb-128gb --desktop "<note>"
argv: --ctx-size 8192 --threads 10 --batch-size 2048 --ubatch-size 2048 --jinja --reasoning-format
deepseek -lv 4 -np 1 --spec-type draft-mtp --spec-draft-n-max 2 --cache-ram 8192; a 2,015-token
prompt, 512 generated tokens.

| variant  | build | offload | draft acceptance     | pp tok/s | decode tok/s | load   |
|----------|-------|---------|----------------------|----------|--------------|--------|
| b12110-a | 12110 | 66/66   | 297 / 426 = 0.697    | 775.0    | 43.40        | 7.46 s |
| b11146-a | 11146 | 66/66   | 307 / 407 = 0.754    | 795.8    | 45.32        | 8.03 s |
| b12110-b | 12110 | 66/66   | 297 / 426 = 0.697    | 783.6    | 43.11        | 7.42 s |
| b11146-b | 11146 | 66/66   | 307 / 407 = 0.754    | 784.3    | 45.20        | 8.03 s |

b11146 reproduces #512's 0.754 exactly. b12110 drafts more (426 against 407) and accepts fewer
(297 against 307), so 512 tokens take about 215 target steps instead of about 205 (+4.9 %), which
accounts for the whole 4.4 % decode loss. Prompt processing is within noise; b12110 loads about
0.6 s faster.

variants nomtp-<variant>, same order:

| b11146-b | 11146 | 66/66   | 917.6    | 28.46        | 7.33 s |

Without MTP b12110 decodes as fast as b11146 (+0.8 %) and reads the prompt about 5 % faster. Both
builds drift down about 1 tok/s across the four starts (card warming), evenly, since builds
alternate. MTP's decode gain: 1.56x on b11146, 1.48x on b12110.

Consistent with upstream #30141 (v0.9.0 changed how MTP's hidden state is saved and restored in
checkpoints), which is what the runbook told this leg to look for; the cause is not established.

Evidence: leg729-mtp-<variant>.{json,stderr.log,heaps.csv,run.log} and
leg729-nomtp-<variant>.{json,stderr.log,heaps.csv,run.log}; leg729-clock-state.csv. In the
run.log files the scratch paths are replaced with <drive> and <out>. heaps.csv is empty: vulkaninfo
is not installed on this machine.
